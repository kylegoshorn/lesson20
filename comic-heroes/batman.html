Who Is Batman?

Batman is the secret identity of Bruce Wayne. He protects Gotham City by using detective skills, careful planning, and advanced technology.

Why I Chose Him

I chose Batman because he shows that a person does not need superpowers to become a hero. His intelligence and preparation are his greatest strengths.
