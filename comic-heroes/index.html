What Makes Someone a Hero?

A hero is not defined only by special powers. Heroes choose to help people, face difficult problems, and keep trying when things go wrong.

Traits of a Great Hero

  • Courage when facing danger
  • Kindness toward people who need help
  • Responsibility for their choices
  • Determination to never give up

THE GREAT COMIC DEBATE

Why DC Is Better Than Marvel

Marvel has many entertaining heroes, but I think DC is better. DC's heroes often feel like modern legends. Superman represents hope, Batman shows the power of preparation, and Wonder Woman stands for truth and peace.

DC also has famous places such as Gotham City, Metropolis, and Themyscira. Each setting has its own mood and feels like an important part of the story.

DC Wins Because It Has:

  1. Iconic heroes who represent powerful ideas
  2. Memorable villains like the Joker and Lex Luthor
  3. Legendary fictional cities and worlds
  4. Stories that can feel epic, mysterious, or hopeful

This is my opinion. Marvel fans are still welcome!
