Who Is Wonder Woman?

Wonder Woman is also known as Diana of Themyscira. She has incredible strength and speed, but she also uses compassion and truth to solve problems.

Why I Chose Her

I chose Wonder Woman because she believes that real strength should be used to create peace. She is brave in battle but also tries to understand other people.
