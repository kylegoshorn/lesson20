Who Is Spider-Man?

Spider-Man is the secret identity of Peter Parker. He can climb walls, sense danger, and swing through the city with webs while trying to balance hero work with everyday life.

Why I Chose Him

I chose Spider-Man because he makes mistakes but tries to learn from them. He understands that having great abilities also means having a responsibility to help others.
