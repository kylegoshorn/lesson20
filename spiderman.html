Who Is Spider-Man?

Spider-Man is the secret identity of Peter Parker. He can climb walls, sense danger, and swing through New York while balancing hero work with everyday life.

Challenges and Victories

  • Learned from Uncle Ben's death that great power brings great responsibility.
  • Continued protecting people while facing school, work, and money problems.
  • Defeated enemies such as the Green Goblin and Doctor Octopus.
  • Saved New York many times even when the public misunderstood him.

Why I Chose Him

I chose Spider-Man because he makes mistakes but learns from them. Even though he is a Marvel hero, his responsibility and determination deserve respect.
